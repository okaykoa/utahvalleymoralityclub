title: "The Problem of Abortion and the Doctrine of Double Effect"
author: Philippa Foot (1967)
category: For the Young Reader
order: 3
---
The essay that gave the world the trolley and its five unlucky workmen.
Foot did not intend a parlor game; she intended to show that our
firmest intuitions about killing and letting die pull in different
directions depending on how a case is framed, and offered the
distinction between negative and positive duties as her own answer &mdash;
one that has convinced remarkably few of her successors completely.
Bring it to a supper discussion at your own risk &mdash; it rarely ends
where it starts.
