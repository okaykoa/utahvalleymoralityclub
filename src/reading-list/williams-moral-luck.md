---
title: "Moral Luck"
author: Bernard Williams (1976)
category: For the Young Reader
order: 2
---
A short essay with a long afterlife. Williams asks us to imagine a lorry
driver who, through no fault of his own, runs down a child who darts into
the road &mdash; and to notice that he will feel something a merely careful
driver who hit nothing never will, some particular agent's regret, even
though neither man did anything wrong. We recommend it to new members not
for the answer it gives, since it hardly gives one, but for how cleanly it
shows that our sense of who owes what to whom is not entirely up to us,
or to them.
